Event & Competition Details

Workshop Experience

Prism & Pigment: The Fluid Art Showcase

📍 Seminar Hall | 📅 Oct 22 | ⏰ 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM

Step into an expressive masterclass led by celebrated artist Ananya R. Learn the science of fluid density, pigment blending, and dirty pours. Every registered participant receives an individual canvas kit to create and take home their artwork.

Click for Featured Artist Profile & Workshop Info ▼

Ananya R.

Lead Visual Artist & Contemporary Colorist

Ananya R. is an acclaimed contemporary fluid artist with over 8 years of studio experience in acrylic flow, resin pouring, and large-scale canvas installations. Her signature "Prism Spectrum" style has been featured in top design galas across Asia.

Masterclass Coverage: Acrylic density ratios, blow-torch cells, varnish sealing.

Take-Home Kit: 12x12 Studio Canvas, 6 Pouring Paints, Protective Apron & Gloves.
